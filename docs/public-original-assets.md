## Concrete alternatives — proposed only

BGJar offers [Wave](https://bgjar.com/wave) and [Stacked Wave](https://bgjar.com/stacked-wave) for light/dark responsive wave variants. Its [license](https://bgjar.com/posts/license/) permits commercial use on the free CC BY 4.0 plan with attribution and lists paid options without attribution. They would change the supplied background appearance and may require a credit. No purchase, download, license acceptance or substitution has been performed; they cannot silently replace the originals under the owner's no-design-change instruction.

Locating the source/license for the existing originals preserves their appearance exactly. The local review candidate may contain unresolved backgrounds for private inspection; the publication gate explicitly refuses them. A successful build/runtime test is not a public-rights approval.
